Finding the slick is only the beginning.

A dark SAR feature is not automatically oil. Environmental conditions create look-alikes, detected slicks drift over time, origin zones are inherently uncertain, and AIS streams may have gaps. A nearby vessel is never automatically responsible.

OceanTrace enforces a rigorous, explainable 6-stage investigation workflow:

Detection → Validation → Drift Reconstruction → Vessel Correlation → What-If Sensitivity → Evidence Dossier
